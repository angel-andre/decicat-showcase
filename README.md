# DECICAT

A one-touch pixel-art runner. Hop across candlestick charts, stomp bears, outsmart a boss, and ride a rocket to the moon.

## How to play

Decicat runs on its own. Your only job is to jump.

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Tap, click, Space, Up arrow, W or Enter | Jump |
| Hold the jump | Jump higher |
| Tap again in mid-air | Double jump |
| M | Mute or unmute |
| P or Esc | Pause |

Green candles are safe to stand on. Red candles hurt, but brushing past one earns a close-call bonus. Land on a bear from above to stomp it. Fall into a gap and the run is over. Your score is the distance you cover plus everything you pick up along the way.
